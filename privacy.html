Wanderly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: you sign in with Google, so Wanderly never sees your password. We keep your name, email, profile photo and what you do in the app (projects, places, votes and chat) so your group can plan a trip together. Only people in your project can see that project. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Who runs Wanderly

Wanderly is a free trip-planning app run by an individual developer (“the organiser”, “we”). It isn't a company. See Contact for how to reach us.

What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
From your Google account: your name, email address, profile photo and a Google user IDTo sign you in, show who you are to your group, and make sure each person votes once
Your sign-in record: when you first signed in and when you last signed inKept by Google's sign-in service to run your account
Projects you create or join: the project name and who is in it (name and photo)To show your projects and who is in each one
Places you add: name, location on the map, description, things to do, and your name as the person who suggested itTo show the places on your group's map
Your votes (yes or no for each place), with your name and photoTo count votes and show who wants to go where
Chat messages you send, when you sent them, and any reports of messagesTo run your group's chat and keep it safe
Itinerary stops (places, dates, times, meeting points, notes) and reservation checklist items, including your name when you tick one offTo plan the trip with your group

We do not collect your password, phone number, device location (GPS) or payment details. We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics tools, and we never sell your information.

Who can see your information

  • People in the same project can see that project's places, votes and chat, and the names and photos of its members. People in your other projects can't.
  • The organiser can access the database to run the app, fix problems and respond to reports or requests.
  • Our service providers (below) process information on our behalf.

What your browser stores

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in until you sign out.
  • Your map choice (Simple or Detailed).
  • A copy of the MRT line map, so it loads faster. It refreshes every 30 days.

These stay on your device and we can't see them. Wanderly doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

  • Places, votes, chat messages, itinerary stops and reservations are deleted automatically 12 months after they were last saved.
  • A project is deleted automatically 12 months after anyone last opened it.
  • When you leave a project, your vote there is removed. Your messages and places stay for the group unless you delete them.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed straight away (see below).

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: on the Projects page, use Delete my account. This removes your votes and chat messages from every project, removes your name from places you added, takes you out of every project, and deletes your sign-in record. Projects where you're the only person are deleted too.
  • Delete a message: use Delete under any message you sent.
  • Leave a project: in the project's Project tab.
  • See or correct your information: contact us and we'll help. Your name and photo come from your Google account, so changing them there updates them here the next time you join a project.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using Wanderly and delete your account.

Keeping it safe

Data is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts it. Security rules make sure only signed-in members of a project can read that project, and people can only change their own votes and send messages as themselves. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't post sensitive information such as passport, bank or ID numbers in the chat. If a data breach affects you, we'll tell you as soon as we can.

Children

  • Wanderly is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
  • People aged 13 to 17 may use Wanderly only with a parent's or guardian's permission. Everyone confirms their age before signing in.
  • If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has signed up without permission, or is under 13, contact us and we'll delete their account and data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For bigger changes, we'll also let you know in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the organiser of Wanderly, FAZ, by email: contact email to be added.
